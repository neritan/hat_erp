# HAT Product & Service Categories

Source: `Pema e produkteve ERP.doc` ("ERP Product Tree", Gladiola Dona, 2026-09-26).
Albanian names are the source of truth; English is a working translation.
Three levels: **Category → Subcategory → Group**, followed by example items.

> Numbering note: in the source, "Pjata, Gota & Servise" is numbered 3.3 but sits under
> category 2, and 3.3 is also used for furniture. It is listed here as **2.4**. Confirm with HAT.

## 1. USHQIM & PIJE — Food & Beverage

### 1.1 Pije me Alkool & Të Ftohta — Alcoholic & Cold Drinks
- **Pije me Alkool të Lartë** (Spirits): Whisky, Vodka, Rum, Gin, Tequila, Rakia, Likere
- **Vera & Shampanjë** (Wine & Champagne): red, white, rosé, sparkling, prosecco
- **Birra** (Beer): bottle, can, draft keg, craft beer
- **Pije Jo-Alkoolike** (Soft Drinks): water (still/sparkling), fruit juice, energy drinks, soda

### 1.2 Kafe, Çaj & Baza e Barit — Coffee, Tea & Bar Basics
- **Kafe** (Coffee): beans, ground, capsules, decaf
- **Çajra** (Tea): bagged, loose-leaf
- **Shurupë & Pure** (Syrups & Purées): cocktail/coffee syrups, fruit purées for smoothies & margaritas
- **Acesorë Pijesh** (Drink Accessories): sugar (white/brown/stevia), coffee milk, cocoa

### 1.3 Ushqime të Freskëta & Të Ngrira — Fresh & Frozen Food
- **Mish & Shpendë** (Meat & Poultry): beef, lamb, pork, chicken, turkey (fresh/frozen, cuts)
- **Peshk & Meze Deti** (Fish & Seafood): fresh/frozen fish, shrimp, squid, octopus, mussels
- **Brumëra & Bukë** (Dough & Bread): baked/par-baked bread, frozen dough (croissant, pizza)
- **Fruta & Perime** (Fruit & Vegetables): fresh, organic, frozen (IQF), pre-cut

### 1.4 Bulmet & Ushqime të Thata — Dairy & Dry Goods
- **Bulmet & Vezë** (Dairy & Eggs): cheeses (soft, hard, mozzarella), butter, cream, eggs
- **Ushqime të Thata & Salca** (Dry Goods & Sauces): oils (olive, sunflower), vinegar, sauces (ketchup, mayonnaise, mustard), spices, flour, rice, pasta
- **Desserts & Ëmbëlsira** (Desserts): ice cream, crêpe/waffle bases, frozen sweets, cooking chocolate

### 1.5 Produkte të egra, artizanale, fshati — Wild, Artisanal & Village Products
Free-range poultry, village dairy, artisanal pastry, wild forest fruit, wild mushrooms, truffle, mountain herbs & spices

### 1.6 Produkte Premium nga e Gjithë Bota — Premium Products Worldwide
Truffles & fine mushrooms; premium meats & charcuterie; caviar & gourmet seafood (sturgeon, Beluga); sushi-grade fish; gourmet cheeses & artisanal dairy; rare spices, salts & saffron; fine pastry

## 2. PAJISJE KUZHINE, ENË & SHËRBIM RESTORANTI — Kitchen Equipment, Cookware & Restaurant Service

### 2.1 Pajisje Industriale Gatimi & Ftohjeje — Industrial Cooking & Refrigeration
- **Furra & Skara** (Ovens & Grills): convection ovens, combi steamers, pizza ovens, gas/electric stoves, grills, fryers
- **Frigoriferë & Prodhues Akulli** (Refrigeration & Ice Makers): upright/chest fridges, cold rooms, ice machines (cube/flake)
- **Sous-Vide & Vakum**: sous-vide units, industrial vacuum packing
- **Larëse Industriale** (Industrial Washers): hood/basket dishwashers, glasswashers

### 2.2 Enë Kuzhine & Aksesorë Gatimi — Cookware
- **Tenxhere, Tigaie & Tava GN** (Pots, Pans & GN Trays): stainless/aluminium pots, induction pans, trays, buffet/oven containers
- **Thika & Vegla Prerëse** (Knives & Cutting Tools): HACCP colour-coded knives, cutting boards, cleavers

### 2.3 Pajisje Shërbimi & Karroca — Service Equipment & Trolleys
- **Chafing Dishes & Bufete**: gas/electric food warmers, bain-maries, buffet displays
- **Termusë & Dispensers**: large water/coffee thermoses, juice dispensers
- **Karroca Industriale** (Trolleys): multi-tier service trolleys, hotel luggage trolleys

### 2.4 Pjata, Gota & Servise — Tableware *(numbered 3.3 in source)*
- **Porcelan & Pjata** (Porcelain & Plates): main plates, appetizer plates, soup bowls, sauce bowls
- **Gota & Shishe** (Glassware): water, wine, cocktail, coffee/tea glasses, carafes
- **Servise** (Cutlery): knives, forks, spoons (dining, dessert, coffee)

## 3. MOBILJE, TEKSTILE & AKOMODIM — Furniture, Textiles & Amenities

### 3.1 Tekstile Stafi, Restoranti & Dhome — Staff, Restaurant & Room Textiles
- **Uniforma Stafi** (Staff Uniforms): kitchen, waiter shirts & aprons, reception & housekeeping, anti-slip work shoes
- **Tekstile Dhome & Fjetje** (Bedding): sheets, pillowcases, decorative throws, blankets, bedspreads
- **Tekstile Banjoje** (Bath Textiles): hand/body towels, bathrobes, bath mats
- **Tekstile Restoranti** (Table Linen): tablecloths, runners, cloth napkins, chair covers, buffet skirting

### 3.2 Dyshekë, Jastëkë & Fjetje — Mattresses, Pillows & Sleep
- **Dyshekë & Mbrojtës** (Mattresses & Protectors): orthopaedic/anatomic, memory foam, elastic/waterproof protectors
- **Jastëkë & Jorgane** (Pillows & Duvets): feather, microfibre, memory foam pillows; protectors; summer/winter duvets; covers

### 3.3 Mobilje Hotele, Restorante & Bare — Hotel, Restaurant & Bar Furniture
- **Mobilje Dhome & Akomodimi** (Room Furniture): beds (single, double, metal/wood, baby), extra/portable beds, wardrobes, nightstands, desks, mirrors
- **Divane & Ndenjëse** (Sofas & Seating): 2/3-seat sofas, sofa beds, lobby armchairs
- **Mobilje Restoranti & Bari** (Restaurant & Bar Furniture): tables, dining chairs, bar stools, outdoor furniture

### 3.4 Amenitete & Pajisje Dhome — Guest Amenities
- **Produkte Higjienike** (Toiletries): shampoo, shower gel, logo soap, disposable slippers, hygiene kits
- **Pajisje Elektrike Dhome** (Room Appliances): minibars, wall hair dryers, room safes, kettles, luggage racks

### 3.5 Materiale Pastrimi & Konsumueshëm — Cleaning & Consumables
- **Kimikate & Detergjente** (Chemicals): dishwasher detergent, rinse aid, degreaser, disinfectant, floor/glass cleaners, pH-specific surface cleaners
- **Produkte Letre** (Paper): industrial toilet paper, hand towels (roll/Z-fold), kitchen paper
- **Mjete Pastrimi** (Cleaning Tools): buckets, vacuums, brooms, squeegees, window tools, cleaning trolleys, bins, sprayers, mops, microfibre cloths

## 4. TEKNOLOGJI, HARDUER & SOFTUER ERP/POS — Technology, Hardware & Software

### 4.1 Harduer — Hardware
- **Pikë Shitjeje (POS)**: touchscreen POS terminals, receipt printers, cash drawers
- **Pajisje Akomodimi** (Accommodation Devices): RFID key cards, card readers, document scanners
- **Rrjeti & Siguria** (Network & Security): guest Wi-Fi routers, CCTV, smart locks

### 4.2 Softuer — Software Licences & Integrations
- **Sisteme Menaxhimi** (Management Systems): bar/restaurant POS, hotel PMS, Channel Manager
- **Integration Module**: ERP modules … *(incomplete in source)*

## 5. SHËRBIME PROFESIONALE, PROJEKTIM & KONSULENCË — Professional B2B Services

### 5.1 Arkitekturë, Projektim & Dizajn — Architecture & Design
- **Projektim Arkitektural & Interior Design**: concept, 3D design, furnishing for hotels, restaurants, bars
- **Projektim Teknologjik Kuzhine/Bari** (Kitchen/Bar Engineering): workflow, equipment layout, HACCP layout
- **Projektim & Realizim** (Design & Build): pools, salt rooms, steam rooms, saunas, playgrounds, water parks

### 5.2 Auditues, Mystery Shopper & Çertifikime — Audits & Certification
- **Mystery Shopper & Auditim Shërbimi**: covert service/staff/food/cleanliness assessment with reports
- **Çertifikime Standardesh**: HACCP, ISO 22000, ISO 9001, hotel star classification

### 5.3 Siguri Fizike/Teknike & Punime Elektrike — Security & Electrical
- **Shërbime Sigurie** (Security): guards, 24/7 monitoring, alarm & CCTV installation/maintenance
- **Punime & Mirëmbajtje Elektrike** (Electrical): industrial electricians, high-power installations, emergency service

### 5.4 Konsulencë, Trajnime — Consulting & Training
- **Konsulencë Operacionale HoReCa**: menu engineering, food cost control, staff management
- **Menaxhim Operacional** (Operational Management): managing accommodation, bars, restaurants
- **Trajnim Stafi** (Staff Training): sommelier, barista, front desk; menu & promo design/print

### 5.5 Lavanteri, Pastrim & Kopshtari — Laundry, Cleaning & Gardening
- Textile laundry, window, façade and parking cleaning
- Gardening, tree care

### 5.6 Shërbime Marketingu — Marketing
Social media management, photo shoots, video production, branding & design, outdoor (billboards, LED screens)

### 5.7 Evente & Artistë — Events & Artists
- **Rezervo Artistin** (Book an Artist): singers, folk groups, dance groups, musicians, dancers, entertainers
- Event organisers, florists
- Equipment rental: audio, lighting, projection
